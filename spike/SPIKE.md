# SPIKE — not the design

Built on 2026-10-21 before the survey and planning phases, from three public repositories only
(team-croffle/croffle, pipit-bot, croffle-play). It is a reference prototype:

- Use it to check whether a design idea runs, not as the design itself.
- Decisions recorded with it are provisional until the design phase reviews them.
- Runs from this directory: `python3 harness.py migrate <repo> --dry-run`,
  `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests`.
